## What's new

- **Weather.** A new app: the weather now, hour by hour, and the next five days, for
  your home and up to three more places. Six themes come with it (Scope, Vintage brass,
  Blueprint, Paper, Celestial, Station), each with its own icons, and its editor makes
  more. Add places on Orb Manager's **App settings** page: type a town and pick it.
- **One dial per app.** Turning the knob moves through what the app shows, a press is the
  app's one action, and a hold opens the **app list** (the apps, then the orb's settings).
  A quick spin always stops on the app's home view, and 20 seconds idle returns there.
  The user guide has a table for each app.
- **Units.** Choose °C or °F, km/h, mph or knots, and km, miles or nautical miles once,
  on the orb's **UNITS** page or Orb Manager's Orb tab. Weather and Flights use them.
- **Flights** shows its range in your distance unit; turning steps through the aircraft
  shown, nearest or farthest first, and a press changes the range.
- **Finding a town.** The place search in Orb Manager (Home, new orbs, and the weather's
  places) now knows every town of 15,000 people or more, with its state or province:
  "Traverse City, MI".

## Fixed

- **Macs could not set up a new orb.** Orb Manager never recognised an orb plugged into a
  Mac, so **New orb** never appeared. It does now.
- **The radar's sweeping arm** in Flights could vanish or draw in pieces.

## Before you update

- Update the firmware first: Weather and Flights need this release's firmware, and Orb
  Manager says so if an orb is behind.
- The knob works differently (above). The carousel of apps is gone: hold the knob for the
  app list.
