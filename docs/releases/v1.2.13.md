## What's new

- **Installs show their steps.** Installing or updating an app or a theme now shows a numbered
  list: "Step 4 of 7 · Restarting the orb to load it". Each step gets a tick and how long it
  took. A step only the orb can finish (installing, storing, restarting) shows a spinner
  and a clock instead of a bar sitting at 100%. The window stays open until the orb is
  back, then says **Done**.
- **Themes show where they are.** A theme install shows its own steps: copying to the SD
  card, checking the file, making room in the orb's flash (when other themes have to
  move), moving it into flash, and restarting.
- **Setting up a new orb** shows the whole way from writing the firmware to the clock on
  the glass as one list, and ends with a green **Your orb is ready**.
- **Firmware updates stand out.** The Firmware tab shows the version an orb runs and the
  one Orb Manager carries. When an update is waiting it gets the same amber as app and
  theme updates: a count on the tab, an **update available** chip and an **Update
  firmware** button. The image details are under **Technical details**.

## Fixed

- **The progress window could fail to appear.** After setting up a new orb, the next install
  greyed the page with no window on it until the page was reloaded.
- **A slow theme store looked like a failure.** While the orb was busy making room, the
  progress line said it had stopped answering. It now says that is expected.
- **An orb erased with Leave it lonely kept its old themes**, and the first theme on the
  reborn orb then spent a minute moving them around. The erase clears them now.

## Before you update

- Update an orb's firmware to see the theme steps; on older firmware the whole store shows
  on one step.
