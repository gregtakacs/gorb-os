## What's new

- **A new way to switch apps.** Hold the knob (or turn it on the clock) and the screen
  zooms down into a row of your apps, each shown by its theme's picture, with the next
  ones peeking in at the sides and a gear for the orb's settings at the end. Turn to move
  along; the row wraps around. Press, or just wait three seconds, and the app you picked
  zooms up to fill the screen and fades into the live app.
- **A hold now works while you are still holding.** The orb answers a hold as soon as it
  passes about two thirds of a second, instead of waiting for you to let go.

## Fixed

- **Macs could not flash an orb.** Orb Manager on a Mac found the orb and then stopped
  partway through writing the firmware with "no response from target". Flashing from a
  Mac works now.
- **"No card in the socket" with a card in it.** When the orb cannot use its SD card it
  now says why: that no card answered (push it in fully, or try another), or that the
  card answered in a format the orb cannot read (it needs FAT32 on a Master Boot Record
  partition). The reason also shows when an install is refused for it.
- **The USB console picked the wrong port** on a computer with more than one serial
  device. It now opens on the orb's own cable.

## Before you update

- Update each orb's firmware to get the new app switcher; Orb Manager shows the update in
  amber on the Firmware tab.
