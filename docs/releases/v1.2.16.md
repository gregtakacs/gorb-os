## What's new

- **A livelier Weather.** The Now view moves: rain, drizzle, thunder, snow, mist, drifting
  cloud and night stars, every icon gently bobbing, a clock in the place's own local time,
  and the next sunrise or sunset. The five days get a highlight on the chosen day, and
  below the list its condition, rain total, strongest wind, UV and sunrise and sunset.
- **Arrange the weather yourself.** In the Weather editor, drag the parts of Now and Places
  into place (or set their angle and distance), size each one on its own, and pick a
  ground from the clock dial's colours, finishes and wear, or a picture of your own. The
  preview plays the weather as it will look, with a picker to try each kind.
- **12-hour or 24-hour time.** Set it with the units, on the orb's UNITS page or in Orb
  Manager; the apps follow it.

## Fixed

- **Installing an app could fail to set its theme**, with "store job refused:
  ESP_ERR_INVALID_STATE", when the install landed in the first seconds after the orb
  restarted. It goes through now.
- **Weather showed a blank picture in the app switcher.** It shows its theme now.
- **A part of a theme could keep its old picture** after going back to its first frame.

## Before you update

- Update each orb's firmware: the app switcher, 12-hour time and the fixes above are in
  the firmware. Weather 1.2.0 needs at least v1.2.14, and Orb Manager says so if an orb
  is behind.
