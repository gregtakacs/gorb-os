## What's new

- **Separate tick and chime volumes for the clock.** Two clock settings, Tick volume and
  Chime volume, set how loud each is under the orb's own volume. Ticks start quieter than
  the chimes (70% against 100%).
- **Your own sound for every chime.** In the clock editor every sound (each tick, the hour
  and quarter chimes, and the rest) has its own upload and a **Back to built-in** button.
  The editor warns when your sounds make the theme large.
- **Order the apps and let them take turns.** Set the app switcher's order on Orb
  Manager's Apps tab (Carousel). Turn on **Rotate apps** there or on the orb (APPS) to
  move from app to app on a timer, and choose which apps take part.
- **Full-resolution theme previews.** Previews are now the full screen, stored
  compressed, so fine print in Weather and Flights themes is readable in the switcher and
  the Themes list.
- **A smoother switcher and Themes list.** Turning through the app switcher no longer
  waits on the SD card. The Themes list loads the previews next to the one you are on, so
  you can turn through them without each one stopping to load.
- **One place for each app on the orb.** APPS, then an app, holds its settings, with
  Themes and Permissions as their own sub-menus. Orb Manager's App settings page shows
  every app the same way.
- **Pressure and lightning in Weather.** Now shows the pressure and its three-hour trend,
  and thunderstorms flash lightning. Pressure is a unit of its own (hPa, inHg, mmHg).
- **Units from your first home.** A new orb picks metric or imperial from the country of
  the first home you set.

## Changes

- **"Build a watch" from scratch starts with the lighting As designed.**
- **The separate PERMISSIONS page, the Themes row and "show on the dial" are gone.**
  Everything moved under each app's own page.
- **A new orb set up without an SD card** now finishes setup with a note instead of an
  error: put a card in, then press Install on Clock on the Apps tab.

## Fixed

- **A slow turn just after pressing the knob could open the app list.** Turning while the
  knob is held down no longer counts as a hold.
- **Reinstalling an app that was running could crash the orb.**
- **Uninstalling an app from the orb could crash it**, and an install or uninstall could
  leave a "? APP" page with only a back row.
- **The Themes list could sit on "working..."** until the knob was turned, and a page
  could stay blank after a card read until the next turn.
- **Weather could show "no forecasts yet" for minutes** after a settings change, when a
  connection the server had closed was reused.
- **A newly installed app had no settings** until the orb restarted.

## Before you update

- Update each orb's firmware to v1.2.20 first: Clock 1.2.29, Flights 1.4.1 and Weather
  1.4.1 need it, and Orb Manager says so if an orb is behind. Apps keep their settings
  across the update.
- Themes made before this release keep their smaller previews. To get a full-resolution
  preview, open the theme in its editor and send it to the orb again.
