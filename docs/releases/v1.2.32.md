## What's new

- **A Radar app.** Rain radar over a map of your home or one of your places. Turn the
  knob to step back through the last frames and forward into the short-range forecast;
  press for the next place; press and turn to zoom, through six steps from Wide to Close.
  Its Lightest rain setting hides the weakest echoes (Drizzle and up by default). It
  ships with the Radarscope theme.
- **Places beside home.** Set up to three places as well as home, on Orb Manager's Orb
  tab (Places). They belong to the orb, not to one app: Weather and Radar both use them.
- **Maps for your places.** The Places card has a Maps table. Make maps builds a map of
  each place on the hub and puts it on the orb's card; it turns amber when the maps are
  out of date. The map credit shows on the glass for a few seconds, and ABOUT lists the
  map and weather sources.
- **A theme carousel.** An app's Themes row now opens a vertical carousel of its
  themes: the one you are on large with its name, the others above and below, and a Back
  card at the top. Turn through the whole list; each picture fills in when you stop, with
  a progress ring while it loads. Press to wear a theme, press the one you already wear
  to go back to the app, or hold to go back.
- **Drawn cards.** The Settings and Back cards are drawn icons, and HSB has its own
  colour-wheel icon in the app switcher.

## Changes

- **The orb starts on the first app** in the carousel after a restart. The Settings
  screen (now titled SETTINGS, not MENU) only shows on its own when no app is installed.
- **Weather's places moved** to the orb's Places. Weather 1.5.0 reads them from there,
  so its own place settings are gone; set them again under Places if you had them.
- **Press and turn** is something an app can use now (Radar zooms with it). A press on
  its own works as before.

## Fixed

- **On a Mac, Orb Manager could not find orbs on the network** ("no LAN listener: no
  interface could join 224.0.0.251") and an orb connected only with Add by IP. Allow
  Orb Manager when macOS asks to find devices on your local network.
- **Theme pictures could stay grey** in the switcher and Themes list, and the list could
  wait a long time after a turn before it moved.
- **The Settings card in the app switcher could show a black MENU picture** instead of
  its icon.

## Before you update

- Update each orb's firmware to v1.2.32 first: Radar 1.2.0 needs it, Weather 1.5.0 needs
  v1.2.30 and HSB 1.3.1 needs v1.2.21. Orb Manager says so if an orb is behind.
- For Radar, set at least home (or a place) and press Make maps on the Orb tab's Places
  card once the orb is updated.
