## What's new

- **Names in their own letters.** Place names, home and the towns on the radar's map show
  their accents: Győr, Tatabánya, Zürich, München, Kraków. This covers the letters of most
  Latin-alphabet languages (Hungarian, German, Polish, Czech, the Nordic languages, French,
  Spanish, Romanian and more); a few are spelled plain (ß as "ss", ø as "o", ł as "l").
  Places and home can now be named with accents on Orb Manager's Orb tab.
- **A smoother Radar.** With the loop playing, the radar redraws about twice as fast, and
  it no longer spends time on the hidden corners of the round screen.
- **Drag to order your apps.** The Apps tab is one list: the apps on the orb in the order the
  knob walks them, each moved by dragging its ⠿ grip. **Rotate apps** and how long to wait sit
  above it; while rotation is on, each app has a checkbox for whether it takes part.
- **Update everything.** When the orb is behind Orb Manager, a strip above the tabs says what
  is out of date, and one press updates the firmware and then every app, in one progress panel.

## Changes

- **Radar maps are read from the card as they are drawn**, not kept in memory. A zoom or a
  place change takes about a second longer to show its map; in exchange the orb no longer
  runs out of room for it.
- **Radar themes can draw rings or a cross under the rain** (a theme setting). The
  Radarscope theme keeps its rings above the rain.

## Fixed

- **The radar showed "Loading map" for good, or only town names with no map**, on an orb
  with several apps installed. The orb now keeps the screen's big pictures (the radar's
  layers, the clock's, the theme carousel's) in one area of memory of their own, so they no
  longer run short when other apps are installed.
- **"App format 3; this build reads format 4" after updating from v1.2.16.** Orb Manager
  v1.2.32 kept the old Clock, HSB, Flights and Weather in its library, so those apps showed
  red errors with nothing to press, and setting an orb up again stopped at "Sending clock to
  the orb's card". Orb Manager now replaces the old apps when it starts, and any app the orb
  still holds from before shows an amber **Update** that puts the new one on.
- **The Apps tab kept asking for a firmware update** the orb already had. It notices the new
  firmware and redraws.
- **Reordering apps and choosing which ones rotate did not save** on a real orb. They do now.
- **macOS: Orb Manager can find orbs on your network** without Add by IP. Allow it when macOS
  asks to find devices on your local network.

## Before you update

- Update each orb's firmware to v1.2.37 first: Radar 1.2.1, Weather 1.5.1 and Flights 1.4.2
  need it, because their themes now carry the accents. Orb Manager says so if an orb is
  behind, and **Update everything** does it in one go.
- **Stuck on the old apps with v1.2.32?** Install this version and start it once. Then open
  the orb and press **Update everything** (or **Update** on each app). If an orb's setup
  stopped at "Sending clock to the orb's card", do the same.
