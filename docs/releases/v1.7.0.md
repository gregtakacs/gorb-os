## What's new

- **Themes and apps land live.** Installing an app, sending a theme or removing one no longer
  restarts the orb: the app you are on keeps running, and the new look appears when it is
  ready. A copy to the card shows its progress as an arc around the edge of the screen.
- **Many more themes on the orb at once.** Themes now live on the SD card and the orb keeps
  the ones you wear most in its own memory, bringing any other back from the card when you
  turn to it (with its picture and a progress ring while it loads). Sounds play from any app,
  on the glass or not.
- **A faster app carousel.** Its pictures come from the orb's memory as you turn, and a light
  on each card says whether that app's theme is already loaded.
- **Theme editors in Orb Manager.** Clock, Radar, Weather and Flights themes are edited on one
  page in Orb Manager, with live previews on your own home map, a size readout, and keyboard
  undo. Each editor opens on its own, safer web address (open them in Chrome, Edge or
  Firefox; Safari can't open them).
- **Maps in Flights and Weather.** Flights draws your home's map under the radar scope, and
  Weather the shown place's map behind the reading; the theme decides what is drawn (roads,
  water, towns, airports). **Sectional**, a flight chart's look, is new for every app.
- **Flights zooms like Radar.** Press and turn the knob to step through Close, City, Area and
  Local, each twice as far as the one before, with the choice shown large on the screen. The
  distances shown are round numbers from a scale the theme draws, a ring or a bar. A press
  goes to the next place. The Range setting is now where Flights opens.
- **Clock sub-dials with real hands.** A sub-dial's hands are separate, smooth hands (two
  can share a sub-dial), and its face can sit above a hand that sweeps underneath it. All
  hands are now drawn smooth. New **Noir tourbillon** clock template.
- **Radar** keeps more frames on the SD card and plays its loop more smoothly in storms.
- **USB disk mode** is more dependable.

## Changes

- **Flights' Range setting** offers Close, City, Area and Local (it keeps the step you had
  chosen) and only sets where the app opens; a press-and-turn changes it for the moment.
- An airport whose runways aren't in the map data now shows its symbol where a theme draws
  runways, instead of nothing.

## Fixed

- **The orb could restart when you zoomed in Flights.** Fixed.
- **The Triple Calendar's moon showed at new moon.** The moon wheel turns as it should.
- **Themes sent one after another could be refused** with "no room" until a restart. Fixed.
- Several memory leaks and crashes found by a memory sweep, including one that could stop
  Orb Manager, and a screen that could freeze until a restart.

## Before you update

- Use **Update everything** in Orb Manager: it updates each orb's firmware and then every
  app to 1.7.0 in one go.
