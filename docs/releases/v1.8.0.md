## What's new

- **Pomodoro, a new app.** A focus timer with short and long breaks: press to start or pause,
  turn to take minutes off or give them back (never past the length you set, down to 0:00),
  and press and turn to jump between phases. Turning all the way anticlockwise reaches
  **Reset**, which stops the timer and clears its ring. Eight looks, each with its own chimes,
  and its own theme editor in Orb Manager, where you can generate a chime or use your own sound.
- **A ring around the edge, over any app.** The pomodoro shows its time left as a ring at the
  rim of whatever app is on the screen. It drains while a phase runs, breathes while paused
  and flashes when a phase ends, opaque on a black track so it is seen over any app. Its
  colour follows the look, or one you choose, with its own opacity, direction and start.
  Other apps can use the ring too.
- **Double-press anywhere for the timer.** While a pomodoro runs, a double press on any app
  pauses or resumes it, and starts the next phase when one ends; the ring pulses to say it
  went there. A single press, a turn and a press-and-turn stay with the app you are using
  (a single press arrives a moment later while the timer runs). The setting **Borrow the
  press** can limit this to the end of a phase, or turn it off.
- **The looks match across apps.** Every look now has its own art in every app: Weather's
  Silver and Sectional have their own icons, Flights' Station and Sectional their own planes,
  and the clock's Sectional is a flight chart like the others. Each look colours Flights'
  planes by height in its own palette and Radar's rain in its own tones. Small lettering is in
  capitals in every look.
- **Every theme has an overlay colour,** set in each app's editor, which the ring can match.

## Changes

- **The ring breathes smoothly.** It refreshes only the ring itself, about 25 times a
  second, at a small fraction of the cost of redrawing the screen, so apps under it keep
  their pace.
- **Flights zooms the way Radar does:** press and turn clockwise to come closer.
- **Chime and tick volumes are a share of the orb's volume,** never louder than it.

## Fixed

- **A clock's moving gears could stop** while its hands kept going, until you switched apps.
  The gears now keep their memory while the clock is on the screen, and recover by themselves.
- **A setting an app saved could revert a few seconds later** when the app saved two at
  once. Fixed for every app.
- **Double and missing ticks** on clock faces with long tick sounds, such as Skeleton. Fixed.
- **The orb could restart** at start-up when the screen's refresh timing was not measured.
  Fixed.

## Before you update

- **The Pomodoro app needs this firmware** (v1.8.0). Update the orb first, then add the app.
