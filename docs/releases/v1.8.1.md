## Fixed

- **Pomodoro's Station showed no time.** The look draws its own split-flap tiles, and they were
  being painted on top of the digits, so the glass showed the flaps and no numbers. The same
  was true of the big time on Silver and most of Brass, where the bars and the engraving cover
  the figures. Every look's digits are on top again, on every look.
- **Pomodoro's themes got about a third smaller** (its default theme went from 1.69 MB to
  1.07 MB), because each look's art now rides one plane instead of two.

## Changes

- **Each look is one picture, not two.** An app's own art now sits on the same layer as its
  background, under the words, instead of on the layer the orb reserves for its glass. This is
  how the other apps already did it, and it is the rule the next app gets checked against.

## Before you update

- Nothing to do first. Pomodoro 1.8.1 runs on v1.8.0 firmware; update the app from Orb
  Manager's Apps tab.
- **If your orb already holds a Pomodoro theme, it is still the old one.** The fix is in the
  app's editor: open the look, press **Send**, and the orb gets the theme with its digits on
  top.
