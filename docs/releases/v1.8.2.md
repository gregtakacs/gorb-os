## What's new

- **The orb's own page is now its settings.** Open the orb's address in a browser on your
  phone or computer (the address is on the new Phone card, below) and change everything the
  dial's Settings menu has: the orb's name, dimming, units and volume. Each app's settings are
  there too. You can add your **home and up to three places** by searching for a city, typing
  coordinates or tapping a map, and **choose each app's theme** from a carousel of previews.
  Add the page to your phone's home screen and it opens like an app.
- **Maps from your phone.** After you add or move a place, press **Make maps** on that page:
  your phone or computer builds the maps Radar, Flights and Weather draw around it and sends
  them to the orb, in under a minute, with no Orb Manager needed.
- **A Phone card in the carousel**, next to Settings. Choose it and the orb shows a QR code:
  scan it with your phone to open the orb's page without knowing its address.
- **Setting up is just a QR code.** A new orb, or one whose Wi-Fi stopped working, shows a
  code on its screen and nothing else. Scan it, join the orb's hotspot, and the setup page
  opens by itself; pick your network and the orb restarts into your apps. If nobody sets it
  up within 10 minutes, it goes back to trying your network.

## Changes

- **The orb's hotspot is only for setup** now. It no longer opens beside a running dial, and
  the "AP-only" mode is gone: an orb that was left in it joins your Wi-Fi again.
- **A place with no maps yet shows no map,** instead of your home's map.
- **Plus codes are no longer accepted** for locations; use a city, coordinates or the map.
- **The old pages at /timezone, /themes and /apps are gone,** replaced by the settings page.
- The firmware is built on ESP-IDF 5.5.6.

## Fixed

- **Some SD cards were reported missing.** Cards that refuse a checksum command at start-up
  now mount, with checksums on. Thanks to @davidphillips2 for finding and diagnosing it
  (#23, #24).
- **Radar could say "updated 588 min ago"** while the card was busy: its refresh waited behind
  card work and gave up. Fetches now get a quiet card.
- **Radar kept re-reading its frames from the card** on every loop, even with room to keep
  them. It keeps what fits.
- **The orb could restart when its hotspot opened** while it was connected (and very likely
  when a changed Wi-Fi password made it open the rescue hotspot).
- **The orb ran very low on memory during downloads,** which could leave it briefly
  unreachable. It now keeps several kilobytes more free.

## Before you update

- **Update Radar, Flights and Weather to 1.8.1** from Orb Manager's Apps tab after the
  firmware, for the "no map yet" fix and Radar's fixes.
- **Bookmark the orb by its name** rather than its number if your phone can: the settings
  page tells you which works.
