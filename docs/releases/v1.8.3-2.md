## What's new

- **Your own sounds take a quarter of the room.** A tick or chime you add to the clock or the
  pomodoro is now kept in the orb's own compressed form from the moment you add it, so a
  theme carries about four times as much sound in the same space. Sounds can be up to 20
  seconds long.
- **Your orb-os.net library in every editor.** With Orb Manager linked to your orb-os.net
  account, each picture and sound picker offers **Your library…** beside your own files:
  everything your designs on the site use, across every app, ready to pick without
  uploading it again.

## Changes

- **Sound limits say what they are.** A sound over 20 seconds is refused with its length,
  and a theme whose sounds come to more than the orb's 1 MB for an app is refused with how
  far over it is.
- **Clock and pomodoro designs saved with this version need this version or later to
  open.** Designs saved before it open as they always did, and move to the new sound form
  the next time you save them.
