## Fixed

- **The orb's page could stop loading, and the orb stop answering,** with the page open on
  several phones and a computer at once. A phone that locked or walked away mid-load kept
  the orb holding memory for it for minutes, until nothing large could be sent: pages spun,
  and Weather could stop drawing its forecast. The orb now lets go of a stalled phone at
  once, and the page is a third of the size on the air, so it loads faster too.
- **Orb Manager said an exported theme went to your Downloads folder** when your browser may
  have put it somewhere else. It now says the browser saved it, and the Themes and Apps tabs
  show where Orb Manager's library is on your computer.

## Changes

- **For app makers:** the theme bake kit now refuses art on the top overlay layer (plate 1)
  unless it is made as glass, and refuses any plate above it. This is the mistake that hid
  Pomodoro's digits in v1.8.0; it now stops at the bake instead of reaching the orb.
  `docs/app-design-rules.md` has a new **Layers and slots** section.
- **Radar 1.8.2** is the same app, baked with the new rule.

## Before you update

- Update the firmware from Orb Manager. Radar 1.8.2 is optional: nothing changes on the glass.
