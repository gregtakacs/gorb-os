# Flights: add Station and Sectional plane families

## Goal

Step 2 of the `looks` slice (design `docs/specs/looks-design.md` §4, §5, §6; closes the second
bullet of `docs/app-platform-plan.md` §64.4). Flights has six plane families; its `Station` and
`Sectional` *starts* draw the Modern silhouettes today. Add two real plane families — **station**
and **sectional** — generated by `apps/flights/art/make_art.py` exactly like the six (every shape
key, every altitude band, the same 100×100 box, pivot `(50,50)`, nose up, legible at 38 px), point
`station()`/`sectional()` at them, then bump/rebake/ship flights.

## Contract

Modify only: `apps/flights/art/make_art.py` (add families), the generated
`apps/flights/art/{planes/*.svg, art.json, review.html}` and
`apps/flights/editor/flights-art.js` (by re-running `make_art.py`, never by hand),
`apps/flights/editor/flights-bake.js` (start family + `PRESETS`), `apps/flights/version.txt`,
`tools/orb-manager/apps/flights-scope.orbtheme` and `tools/orb-manager/apps/flights.orbapp` (by the
shipping tools). No firmware, no hub Go, no kit.

- In `make_art.py`, add two shape renderers reusing the `MODERN` shape set:
  - `render_station(p, band, uid)` — one crisp silhouette filled with `band`, a hairline of
    `OUTLINE`, and a small `#E3000F` home-marker tick; **no** heavy `OUTLINE_W` casing (design §4a).
  - `render_sectional(p, band, uid)` — a hollow chart symbol: the masked-outline technique of
    `render_blueprint` in chart blue, plus a magenta (`#C2187A`) tick (design §4b).
- Add two `FAMILIES` entries:
  - `'station': ('Station', 'Shape and colour: crisp solid silhouettes on a station's white, one red tick.', MODERN, render_station, '#111111', [])`
  - `'sectional': ('Sectional', 'Shape and colour: hollow chart symbols in chart blue with a magenta tick.', MODERN, render_sectional, '#2A5DB0', [])`
  and two `SINGLES` entries reusing `SINGLE_AIRLINER` with the same renderers; and two `PAIRS`
  entries `station→('chart','needle')`, `sectional→('chart','drafting')`. Do **not** edit the six
  existing families or add a `BACKGROUNDS`/`ARMS` entry.
- Re-run `python3 apps/flights/art/make_art.py`; it must write `planes/station-{light,…,generic,
  single}.svg`, `planes/sectional-…svg`, add the two to `art.json`'s `families`/`planes`, list them
  in `review.html`, and add them to `flights-art.js` — **without** changing any existing family's
  SVG or `art.json` entry (deterministic regeneration).
- `flights-bake.js`: `station()` sets `d.planes.family = 'station'` (`:222` → replace `'modern'`);
  `sectional()` sets `d.planes.family = 'sectional'` (`:201`); `PRESETS` (`:68`) becomes
  `['modern','silver','brass','blueprint','paper','celestial','station','sectional']`. Drop the now
  redundant `START_INFO` override (`:163`) so `startInfo` reads the art's `families[k]`. Keep every
  other field of both starts (ground, arm, bands, words, frames, map) exactly as they are.
- `apps/flights/version.txt`: stays `v1.7.2` (the branch already bumped it for §64.5 and has not been pushed; one bump per push), but the shelf is re-baked and the `.orbapp` rebuilt all the same.

## Behaviour and edge cases

- A `station`/`sectional` design's Planes step now defaults to its own family and every shape key
  renders; the design validator accepts family `station`/`sectional` (they are in `A().families`).
- The two starts stay legible at 38 px (`make_art.py` `PLANE_PX`) across all seven `BANDS`
  (`user-monitor-177dpi.md`, flight-tracker-design §11): no heavy casing (Station) and hollow
  outlines thick enough to survive downsample (Sectional).
- The six existing families and the Scope shelf bake are untouched (the `modern` family and its
  `modern.orbtheme` bake name do not change — only the two *starts*' family).
- If `make_art.py` is not deterministic for the six existing families (their SVGs change), that is a
  bug to fix, not to accept (design §6).

## Notes

- A family's identity is its renderer + `file_band`; reusing `MODERN` for shape is intended (modern
  / silver / blueprint already share it). The point of §64.4 is that Station and Sectional are
  *their own family*, not `planes.family: 'modern'`.
- Take the "before" bake on the base tree (before running `make_art.py` / editing), the "after" once
  everything ships. `FLIGHTS_BAKE_KEEP` dirs must be empty first.
