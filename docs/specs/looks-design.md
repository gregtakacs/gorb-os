# LOOKS design — close the eight-look parity gaps (issue #11, `app-platform-plan.md` §64.4)

Design only. This slice adds the art that closes the two parity gaps §64.4 names — weather's
Silver and Sectional icon packs, and flights' Station and Sectional plane families — removes the
stale old look names, ships both apps, and proves with pictures that the new art belongs to its
look at true size. No product code is written here; the three step specs under this plan are run
one by one by the `looks` chain.

## 1. What the slice adds, mapped to §64.4

§64.4 ("The eight looks, with nothing borrowed") lists four gaps. Two are this slice's, two are
not:

- **Weather's Silver and Sectional get their own icon packs.** Today `weather-bake.js`
  `PRESETS_DEF.silver.icons.pack = 'engraved'` (`:173`) borrows Brass's Engraved pack and
  `PRESETS_DEF.sectional.icons.pack = 'pictogram'` (`:180`) borrows Station's Pictogram pack.
  That is exactly the borrowing weather-plan §0 ruling 6 forbids ("every theme has its own icon
  pack … packs are never shared"). Fix: two new packs, **Guilloché** and **Charted**; Silver and
  Sectional wear them; no pack is shared by two looks afterwards. (§64.4, first bullet.)
- **Flights gains Station and Sectional plane families.** It has six (`flights-bake.js` `PRESETS`
  `:68`: modern, silver, brass, blueprint, paper, celestial). Its `station()` (`:211`) and
  `sectional()` (`:195`) starts draw the **Modern** silhouettes today (`planes.family: 'modern'`
  at `:222`/`:201`). Fix: two new families generated by `make_art.py` like the six others.
  (§64.4, second bullet.)
- **The stale old names go.** Two, both named in §64.4: weather `PRESETS_DEF.scope.match`
  still reads `'Flights · Modern'` (`weather-bake.js:136`) — flights' Scope look was renamed from
  "Modern" in `flights-map-plan.md` §6; and `tools/rebake_shelf.py`'s docstring still calls the
  defaults "Modern for flights" and "Radarscope for the radar" (`:5`, `:7`, `:18`) — radar's Scope
  was renamed from "Radarscope" in the same ruling. (§64.4, third bullet.)
- The pomodoro starting from all eight is **out of scope** here (§64.4 fourth bullet) — this task
  names the pomodoro explicitly under "Out of scope".

`app-design-rules.md` "Looks" is the rule book this implements: every app starts from the same
eight looks in order (Scope, Paper, Brass, Blueprint, Station, Celestial, Silver, Sectional),
each look is its own with nothing borrowed, and the shelf default is Scope.

## 3. New weather icon packs

Both packs are added to `weather-bake.js` in three coupled places, like the six that exist:
a `PACKS` entry (`{title, note, roles}`, `:124`), a `makePack` branch returning the shape object
`{frame?, sun, moon, cloud, rain, drizzle, snow, bolt, fog}` (`:297`), and a `PRESETS_DEF`
`icons.pack`/`icons.c` change (`:168`, `:176`). `iconPaths` (`:385`) already composes them for
all ten `KINDS` (`:26`: clear, clearN, partly, partlyN, cloudy, fog, drizzle, rain, snow,
thunder), so a pack that draws the nine `pk.*` parts covers every condition. `packStart(pack)`
(`:222`) returns the colours of the one starter theme that wears the pack, so a pack worn by a
single theme is self-consistent — the whole point of the change. The 3D `finish`/`SHADOWS` pass
(`:411`) is pack-agnostic and still applies.

### 3a. Guilloché — the Silver look's pack (key `guilloche`, title `Guilloché`)

**Brief to the illustrator.** Engine-turned silver, the finish on a dress watch's sub-dial: a
bright tin-white fill going from a lit top-left to a cool graphite bottom-right, a hairline
engraved outline that catches the light, and a turned (guilloché) pattern where the Engraved pack
would put a flat sunburst. It reads as Silver, not Brass-in-silver, because it is **cool and
single-tone with a specular streak**, while Engraved is warm brass with a chunky 16-ray sunburst
and a deep engraved inner line. Spend **shape + material**; leave colour off (one amber only),
no coloured rims (app-design-rules "Looks").

- Sun: a polished silver disc with an engine-turned ray field — fine concentric turned rings cut
  by 12 slim rays (not Engraved's 16) — and a bright specular crescent at top-left.
- Moon: a silvered crescent, hairline rim, one turned-ring glow.
- Cloud: a solid silver body (the plate `c1`, `#2C3138`) with a bright leading edge and a hairline
  engraved inner arc — the metal reads by its gradient, not a stroke of another colour.
- Rain / drizzle / snow: in the silver body colour — snow a turned six-spoke, drizzle one silver
  bead, rain a slim silver drop.
- Fog: silvered bands, brighter on the top line, like a silvered dial.
- Bolt: the only colour — a single amber strike (`warn`, `#FF9A5C`), no rim.
- Roles (four, so they stay distinct from Engraved's): `metal` (Body, `#C8CED6`),
  `hi` (Highlight, `#FFFFFF`), `deep` (Shade, `#5A626C`), `warn` (Lightning, `#FF9A5C`);
  `PRESETS_DEF.silver.icons.c` carries exactly these four.
- Note shown by the Icons step (`pk.note`):
  `'engine-turned silver; a bright specular streak, one amber bolt'`.

### 3b. Charted — the Sectional look's pack (key `charted`, title `Charted`)

**Brief.** Aeronautical-chart symbology, the symbols printed on a sectional chart: drawn as
**clean hollow linework in chart navy with a magenta accent and a cream paper knockout**, so they
read like the VOR/airport symbols in `radar-bake.js:161` (`PRESETS.sectional`: ring `#2A5DB0`,
track/forecast `#C2187A`, city `#2B2B2B`) and the clock's `Sectional` template
(`watch-engine.js:1067`: chart paper `#eef1e4`, blue rose `#2a5db0`, magenta rings `#c2187a`).
Spend **shape + colour** (navy, magenta); the detail axis is a single hatch only — not all three at
40 px, no rim. It reads as Sectional, not Station-pictogram-recoloured, because the symbols are
**outlined chart glyphs** (a compass-rose sun, a ticked airport circle), whereas Pictogram is solid
filled railway blocks.

- Sun: a chart compass rose — a navy circle with a ticked cardinal cross, the magenta needle.
- Moon: a hollow navy crescent with a hairline.
- Cloud: a navy chart-symbol cloud, cream (`#F3F5EC`, the plate) knockout inside the outline, a
  magenta base tick — an outlined symbol, not a solid block.
- Rain / drizzle / snow: chart precipitation symbology — short navy hatch strokes for rain, one
  navy dot for drizzle, a navy hexagon with a magenta pip for snow (a chart's snow symbol).
- Fog: navy dashed banding, as an area shading.
- Bolt: a magenta strike (the accent).
- Roles (four): `ink` (Chart ink, `#1F2A44`), `accent` (Magenta, `#C2187A`),
  `paper` (Paper knockout, `#F3F5EC`), `warn` (Magenta, `#C2187A`);
  `PRESETS_DEF.sectional.icons.c` carries these.
- Note: `'aeronautical chart symbols; hollow navy linework with a magenta accent'`.

After this, no two looks share a pack: Scope→Phosphor, Paper→Folded, Brass→Engraved,
Blueprint→Drafted, Station→Pictogram, Celestial→Nebula, Silver→Guilloché, Sectional→Charted.

## 4. New flights plane families

`make_art.py` turns each family into: `planes/<fam>-<key>.svg` for every shape key
(`keys(fam)` = the shape set's keys + `single`), an `art.json` `families`/`planes` entry, an entry
in `review.html`, and a block in `editor/flights-art.js` (`art_pack`, `:846`) with `shapes` (band
token `{{B}}`), `plain` (untinted), `band`, `single`, `pair`, `gradients`. A family is
`FAMILIES[<fam>] = (title, band-on text, SHAPE_SET, renderer, file_band, gradients)` plus
`SINGLES[<fam>]` plus `PAIRS[<fam>] = (background key, arm key)`. Adding a family reuses an
**existing** shape set and **existing** `BACKGROUNDS`/`ARMS` keys (no new background/arm); the
family's own identity is its **renderer** and its `file_band`. All on the same 100×100 box, pivot
`(50,50)`, nose up, shown at 38 px (`make_art.py:24`) — the same pivot and size rules as the six.
Legibility is judged at 38 px per flight-tracker-design §11 and `user-monitor-177dpi.md`.

Both new families reuse the `MODERN` shape set (as modern/silver/blueprint already share it) and a
new renderer, so every shape key (light, turboprop, bizjet, airliner, heavy, helicopter, generic —
`flights-bake.js:23` `SHAPES`) and `single` gets its own SVG, and every one of the seven `BANDS`
(`make_art.py:30`) is rendered in review — the same matrix as the six.

### 4a. Station (key `station`, title `Station`)

**Brief.** The railway clock's look at `watch-engine.js:1056` / `radar-bake.js:117` (white ground,
`#111111` black, `#E3000F` red only) rendered as **clean solid silhouettes with a thin light
outline and no material** — the flat, high-contrast black-on-white of a departure board. Spend
**shape + colour** (the band colour is the plane, so the altitude ladder `station()` already sets,
`flights-bake.js:222`, does the reading). It is not `render_modern` again: modern lays a heavy
`OUTLINE_W` (8) dark casing *under* the fill, and that casing is what blurs at 38 px;
`render_station` uses a single crisp fill with a hairline of `OUTLINE` and a red home-marker tick
so the single accent reads. `file_band = '#111111'`; `SINGLES['station']` reuses `SINGLE_AIRLINER`
with the same crisp renderer. `PAIRS['station'] = ('chart', 'needle')` — a valid chart ground and
needle arm for the editor's *initial* controls only; the `station()` start keeps its own white
ground and red line arm, so the shipped look is unaffected.

### 4b. Sectional (key `sectional`, title `Sectional`)

**Brief.** The same chart look as the weather pack: **hollow chart-symbol silhouettes** in chart
blue (`file_band '#2A5DB0'`) — the outline carried by a masked stroke (as `render_blueprint` does
with a mask so overlaps stay out of the line), so the chart ground shows through and the plane reads
as an airport-style symbol, not a solid blob. Spend **shape + colour**. `SINGLES['sectional']`
reuses `SINGLE_AIRLINER` with the same hollow renderer. `sectional()` already sets a magenta/blue
`SECTIONAL_BANDS` ladder (`flights-bake.js:165`), so the band does the altitude reading.
`PAIRS['sectional'] = ('chart', 'drafting')`.

### 4c. Wiring them into the starts (flights-bake.js)

- `station()` (`:211`): `d.planes.family = 'station'` (was `'modern'`). Keeps its own white ground,
  red line arm, black/red words and custom `bands` — only the plane family changes.
- `sectional()` (`:195`): `d.planes.family = 'sectional'` (was `'modern'`); keeps its own chart
  scope, magenta arm, `SECTIONAL_BANDS`.
- `PRESETS` (`:68`) gains `'station'`, `'sectional'` so the editor's **Planes step** Family
  dropdown (`flights-editor.js:432`, which iterates `B.PRESETS`) offers them, and
  `startInfo`/`FAMILY` fall through to the art's `families[k]` (its own `band`/`single`) rather than
  the hand-written `START_INFO` override (`:163`), which becomes redundant once the art is truthful.

## 5. Shipping (requirement 5) — done in the step that last touches each app

Weather is last touched by step 1 (its packs + the Scope `match` fix are the last edits under
`apps/weather/`); flights by step 2; step 3 edits only `tools/rebake_shelf.py`'s docstring, which is
no app, so it needs no bump. Each changed app, in its own step:

1. one `version.txt` bump, **third segment** (app-design-rules "Shipping"): weather
   `v1.7.1 → v1.7.2`, flights stays `v1.7.2` (already bumped on this unpushed branch; orchestrator amendment).
2. re-bake its shelf default with `python3 tools/rebake_shelf.py <slug>` (needs Chromium; writes
   `tools/orb-manager/apps/weather-scope.orbtheme` / `flights-scope.orbtheme`, stamping the new
   version). Required: weather-scope's carried design changes with the `match` string, and the
   bump's `made-with` must move for both apps.
3. rebuild its `.orbapp` with `python3 tools/make_factory_apps.py` (the editor region is packed
   into the container, so any `apps/<slug>/editor/*` edit stales it; `make check-apps` byte-compares).
4. `make check-apps` (with `ORB_CLANG=/usr/lib/llvm-21/bin/clang`) passes.

## 6. The gate, as concrete checks

Each step's verify runs the real toolchain, never a mock. Proven working in this session's
environment: weather `bake_check.py` printed `20 themes baked, 0 failed`; `make check-apps` passes
at baseline; `chromium --headless=new … --screenshot` wrote a PNG; `node --check` passed on both
bake files.

- **Real bakes.** The touched app's `bake_check.py` (headless Chrome; projects every theme through
  `theme_schema` + `orb_clock3.c` + the pool budget) reports `0 failed`.
- **New names, or it fails.** Grep the touched file(s) for the new identifiers with `grep -q` so an
  absent name is a hard failure: weather `pack: 'guilloche'` and `pack: 'charted'` in
  `PRESETS_DEF` and titles `Guilloché`/`Charted`; flights `'station'`/`'sectional'` in `FAMILIES`
  and `planes.family = 'station'`/`'sectional'` in the starts, plus the generated
  `planes/station-*.svg`/`planes/sectional-*.svg` and their `art.json` entries.
- **Nothing borrowed afterwards.** `grep` that `PRESETS_DEF.silver` no longer says
  `pack: 'engraved'` and `.sectional` no longer says `pack: 'pictogram'`; and that all six original
  packs are still worn (phosphor, engraved, drafted, folded, nebula, pictogram each appear as some
  look's `pack:`), so the change added packs rather than swapping them.
- **Stale names gone (step 3).** `grep` for the old strings is empty: no `Flights · Modern` in
  `weather-bake.js`; no `Modern for flights` / `Radarscope` in `tools/rebake_shelf.py`.
- **Existing looks unchanged (requirement 6).** Weather: the five looks that keep their pack
  (`paper, brass, blueprint, station, celestial`) plus `blank` and their bake_check variants bake
  **byte-identical** before/after, proved by `WEATHER_BAKE_KEEP` into two evidence dirs and a
  `sha256sum` diff. The *only* changed `.orbtheme`s are `silver`/`sectional` (the new packs,
  sanctioned) and the Scope-derived names (`scope`, `map-off`, `map-drafting-wide`,
  `scope-wears-nebula`, `largest-moved`), which differ **only** in the carried design's `match`
  string (the sanctioned stale-name edit). Flights: after re-running `make_art.py`,
  `git diff --name-only -- apps/flights/art/planes` lists **only new** `station-*`/`sectional-*`
  files — no existing family's SVG changes — and the flights bake_check keeps the six existing
  looks + custom variants byte-identical, with only the `station`/`sectional` presets changing.
- **Pictures (requirement 4).** Saved under the evidence dir as PNGs (see §7).
