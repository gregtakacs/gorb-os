Pomodoro on the orb

The redesigned looks (docs/specs/pomodoro-looks.md): eight timers, not clocks. They have no minute scales, the digits never shift sideways, and the rim is left to the OS ring. The ring colours are baked into each look. The chimes are synthesized here the way the editor's sound pane would generate them. The art is a sketch of the design, not the real bake.

Knob

Press starts, pauses and resumes. Turn changes the time left, a minute a click. Press+turn picks a phase, shown in the header; it lands when you let go.

Chimes

Each look's chime is generated from a recipe, kept above 350 Hz for the orb's small speaker, and played at the chime volume (a share of the orb's volume). In the editor you could also pick any chime for any phase, or upload your own. Your speakers are much better than the orb's, so these will sound fuller here.

Ring colours aren't settings: each look bakes its own three (focus, short break, long break), editable in the look's editor.
