    Every board the hub has met as an orb gets one tile, keyed on its MAC and followed through address changes. A board on a cable is asked what it is the moment the hub notices it, over the same lead, at no cost to the board: one that answers is a tile. A chip that answers nothing runs no orb and is not listed at all — it is the New Orb button. A tile turns amber when this hub carries newer firmware than that orb runs, and opens that orb when you click it anywhere on the tile. Forget appears only on a board that is not answering, not announcing, and not on a cable; a board you can still reach will re-list itself.

    No orbs yet. A board on a cable that stayed silent when the hub asked it runs no orb: give it one with the New Orb button. Add an orb by its address if the hub cannot hear it announce.
